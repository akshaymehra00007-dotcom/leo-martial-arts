GOOD TO KNOW.

Can I start if I’m new to martial arts?

Contact the academy with your experience and fitness goals. The team can help you choose a suitable training program and explain how to begin.

What are the fees and session timings?

Contact LEO directly for current membership options, class availability, and timings. Call +91 95211 69690 or use the enquiry button below.

Where is the academy?

Plot No. 281, opposite Pooja Tower, Gate No. 1, Muktanand Nagar, Durgapura, Gopalpura Bypass, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302018. Use the directions link below to plan your visit.

What should I bring for my first session?
